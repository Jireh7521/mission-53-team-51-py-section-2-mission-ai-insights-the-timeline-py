1. Foundations	1	Before 1950	Myths and automata of "thinking machines" (e.g., Talos)	Foundations	  The idea of artificial minds is ancient, long before computers.
	            2	1950	Turing publishes "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" and proposes the Turing Test	Foundations	Made machine intelligence testable by behaviour, not consciousness.
	            3	1956	Dartmouth Summer Research Project; McCarthy coins "Artificial Intelligence"	Foundations	Formally launched AI as a research field.
	            4	1956	Logic Theorist proves mathematical theorems	Symbolic AI	Often called the first AI program; machines could reason, not just calculate.
2. Early AI 
and winters	    5	1966	Weizenbaum creates ELIZA	Natural Language Processing	First chatbot; showed how readily people attribute understanding to machines.
	            6	1980s	Expert systems flourish (R1 reportedly saves DEC $25M/year in 1982)	Symbolic AI	First big commercial success of AI, using rules written by specialists.
	            7	1986	Backpropagation makes multi-layer neural networks practical	Neural Networks	The training method behind modern deep learning.
	            8	1980s-1990s	The AI winters	Field History	Hype outran results and funding collapsed, pushing the field toward realism and data.
3. Search, 
data, ML	    9	1997	Deep Blue defeats Kasparov	Game AI / Search	Machines beat the best human at a task seen as intellectual.
	            10	2004-2007	DARPA Grand and Urban Challenges	Robotics / Machine Learning	Proved self-driving was feasible, seeding the autonomous vehicle industry.
	            11	2006-2009	Netflix Prize	Machine Learning	Popularised applied ML and recommendation systems.
	            12	2009	Fei-Fei Li introduces ImageNet	Computer Vision / Data	The labelled dataset that fuelled the deep-learning boom.
	            13	2011	IBM Watson wins Jeopardy!	Natural Language Processing	Handled wordplay-heavy natural-language questions.
4. Deep 
learning era	14	2010s	Deep learning revolution (CNNs, GPUs, big data)	Deep Learning	Dramatically better image recognition, enabling photo tagging and medical imaging.
	            15	2014	Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)	Generative AI	Two competing networks learned to produce realistic content.
	            16	2016	New: AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol 4-1	Game AI / Reinforcement Learning	Beat a Go champion using neural networks plus tree search.
	            17	2017	Transformer architecture ("Attention Is All You Need")	LLMs / Deep Learning	The foundation of modern language models.
5. Apex: 
generative AI	18	2020	New: GPT-3 announced (paper May, API beta June)	LLMs / Generative AI	A 175-billion-parameter model that handled many tasks from a few examples.
	            19	2022	ChatGPT brings generative AI to the mainstream	Generative AI / LLMs	Put AI that writes, translates, summarises and converses in everyone's hands.
	            20	Future	AGI and superintelligence	Future / Speculative	Open questions about AI's long-term direction; still theoretical.
