02 / THE VISUAL NARRATIVE

One layer
builds on another.

The pyramid groups related milestones into five broad eras. Its layers are a learning aid, not a claim that AI developed in a simple straight line or that each approach replaced the previous one.

01

Foundations

Early ideas, the imitation game, the Dartmouth project and symbolic reasoning.

02

Symbolic AI and its limits

Conversational pattern matching, expert systems and periods of reduced AI investment.

03

Learning and applied AI

Neural networks, game search, autonomous vehicles, recommendations, vision and question answering.

04

Generative AI

Generative models, attention-based architectures and widely accessible conversational AI.

05

Open future questions

AGI and superintelligence remain hypothetical concepts, not completed milestones.
